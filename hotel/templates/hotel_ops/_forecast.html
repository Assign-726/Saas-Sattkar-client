How it works

Expected pickup compares each night with how many rooms that weekday usually sells (last 8 weeks) and how many of those bookings normally arrive this late. Suggestions raise rates when the forecast passes 70% and lower them below 45%. Applying one adds a one-night seasonal rate for every room type, which the website and channels pick up.
