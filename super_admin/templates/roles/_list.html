How roles work

  • No accessHidden from the menu. Opening it takes the person to a screen they can use.
  • ViewCan open lists, reports and details, but cannot add, edit, delete, bill or export changes.
  • ManageFull use of that area.
  • Which admin they sign in toRestaurant areas open the Restaurant admin, hotel areas the Hotel PMS, both open either. Every change is recorded in Activity Logs.
